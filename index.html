About Me

How did I end up in AI product?

I started at Accenture as an Application Development Analyst, writing functional and technical specs for Procure-to-Pay and Order-to-Cash in Oracle EBS, running UAT and tracking defects. That taught me how much a product depends on everyone agreeing on what "working" means.

I then completed an M.S. in Data Science for Business at Portland State University, working in Python, SQL, machine learning and Power BI. Now, as an AI Product Intern at Quabyt (Hendrix CX), I turn agentic AI requirements and known failure modes into acceptance criteria and release-readiness checks, and review LLM output against source documents.

The question I keep returning to is what an AI system should do when it isn't confident enough to decide. That leads to clarification, escalation, human review and evaluation. I'm looking for Associate Product Manager and AI product roles.
